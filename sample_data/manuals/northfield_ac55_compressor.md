# Northfield AC-55 Rotary Screw Air Compressor: Maintenance Guide

Fictional sample document written for the EquipTrack demo. Not a real product.

## 1. Alarm codes

A-03 High discharge temperature. Discharge air is above 110 °C. Check the ambient temperature around the
compressor, clean the oil cooler, and check the oil level. The compressor stops automatically at 115 °C.

A-07 Motor overload. The main motor drew too much current. Let the motor cool for 30 minutes, check the supply
voltage, and make sure the inlet valve is not stuck closed.

## 2. Service intervals

Change the air intake filter every 2,000 operating hours, or sooner in dusty areas. Change the oil separator
element every 4,000 hours. Use only Northfield synthetic compressor oil (grade NF-46); never mix mineral and
synthetic oils.

## 3. Condensate

Drain the condensate from the air receiver tank daily, at the start of each shift, using the manual drain valve.
Check that the automatic drain trap cycles every few minutes while the compressor is loaded.
