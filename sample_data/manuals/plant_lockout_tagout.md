# Plant Lockout/Tagout Procedure (all equipment)

Fictional sample document written for the EquipTrack demo.

## Steps

1. Notify the operator and supervisor that maintenance is about to start.
2. Shut the machine down using its normal stop control.
3. Isolate every energy source: electrical disconnect, hydraulic and pneumatic supply, and stored mechanical energy.
4. Attach your personal lock and a tag with your name and the date to each isolation point.
5. Release stored energy (bleed air and hydraulic pressure, block raised parts) and verify zero energy by trying
   the start control.

## Removing locks

Only the person who applied a lock may remove it. If that person has left the site, the supervisor may remove the
lock only after confirming the person is gone and the machine is safe, and must record it in the shift log.
