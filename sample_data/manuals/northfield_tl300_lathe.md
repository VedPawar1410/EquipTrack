# Northfield TL-300 CNC Lathe: Service Manual

Fictional sample document written for the EquipTrack demo. Not a real product.

## 1. Daily start-up

Before the first cycle of the day, check the coolant level, the way-oil reservoir and the chuck pressure gauge.
After a shutdown longer than 48 hours (for example a weekend), warm up the spindle: run it at 500 RPM for 10 minutes,
then at 1,500 RPM for 5 minutes, before cutting. Skipping the warm-up shortens spindle bearing life.

## 2. Alarm codes

E-47 Spindle encoder fault. The controller lost the spindle position signal. Usual causes are a loose encoder cable
at the rear of the headstock, or coolant contamination on the encoder disc. Action: power down, check and reseat the
encoder cable connector, clean the encoder disc with isopropyl alcohol, then restart. If E-47 returns within one shift,
replace the encoder cable (part TL-ENC-04).

E-12 Low coolant pressure. Coolant pressure stayed below 2 bar for more than 10 seconds. Inspect coolant pump P-3,
clean the pump intake filter, and top up coolant to the MAX line.

E-31 Turret not locked. The turret did not reach its locked position within 3 seconds after an index. Check the
turret clamp air pressure (minimum 5.5 bar) and remove chips from the turret coupling.

## 3. Lubrication

Lubricate the slideways every 40 operating hours with ISO VG 68 way oil. The automatic lubricator reservoir holds
2 litres; refill it when the level drops below the MIN mark. Grease the tailstock quill monthly with lithium grease.

## 4. Spindle bearings

A grinding or rumbling noise from the headstock at high spindle speed usually means worn spindle bearings.
Measure spindle runout with a dial indicator: more than 0.01 mm total indicated runout means the bearings need
replacement by a qualified technician. Do not keep running a spindle that is getting hot (above 60 °C at the
headstock housing).

## 5. Chuck and workholding

Torque the chuck jaw mounting bolts to 45 N·m in a crosswise pattern. Grease the chuck jaws weekly through the
grease nipples. Never exceed the maximum chuck speed of 3,500 RPM engraved on the chuck body.
