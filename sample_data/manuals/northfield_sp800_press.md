# Northfield SP-800 Hydraulic Stamping Press: Operator and Maintenance Manual

Fictional sample document written for the EquipTrack demo. Not a real product.

## 1. Ratings

Maximum tonnage: 800 tons. Maximum stroke rate: 18 strokes per minute. Never run a die that needs more than
90 percent of rated tonnage for production work.

## 2. Fault codes

E-47 Die clamp open. One or more hydraulic die clamps are not clamped, so the press will not cycle. Action: close
all die clamps from the clamp panel and confirm every clamp indicator is green before cycling.

H-21 Hydraulic oil overtemperature. Oil temperature is above 65 °C. Stop production, check that the oil cooler fan
is running and the cooler fins are clean, and verify the oil level in the sight glass.

H-09 Hydraulic filter clogged. The filter differential pressure switch has tripped. Replace the return-line filter
element (part SP-FLT-22) and record the change in the maintenance log.

## 3. Two-hand control

The press uses dual palm buttons. Both buttons must be pressed within 0.5 seconds of each other, otherwise the
stroke is blocked (anti-tie-down). Test the two-hand control at the start of every shift: pressing one button alone
must never start a stroke.

## 4. Ram drift

If the ram slowly drifts down while the press is stopped at top dead center, the counterbalance valve is leaking.
Block the ram with the safety blocks, then have the counterbalance valve inspected and resealed. Drift of more than
5 mm in 10 minutes is a stop-work condition.
